    Ready for a trip to the past?

    Meet Tick the clock. Visit 5 stations with stories about teenagers and adults. Pick the right tense and make Tick smile.

    Past simple

    • +My brother played football yesterday.
    • −My parents didn't watch the news last night.
    • ?Did your father work yesterday?

    Past continuous

    • +My sister was playing tennis at 5 o'clock.
    • −The men weren't working at noon.
    • ?Were you sleeping at ten last night?

    While and when

    • •While the teenager was walking, he saw an accident.
    • •My father was working when the phone rang.
    • •I / he / she / it / everyone = was. You / we / they = were.
